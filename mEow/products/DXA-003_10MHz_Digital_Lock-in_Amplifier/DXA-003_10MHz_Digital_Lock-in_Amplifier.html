DXA-003 10MHz Digital Lock-in Amplifier

Overview

The DXA-003 is a high-performance digital lock-in amplifier covering frequencies from DC to 10MHz. It features time constants ranging from 30ns to 4.4ks and offers an exceptional dynamic reserve of over 120 dB. With full-scale sensitivity from 1 nV to 1 V and low input noise down to 6nV/√Hz, it is capable of detecting extremely weak signals. The instrument also incorporates built-in Scope and Spectrum Analyzer functions, a PID controller, and supports AM Modulation, making it a versatile tool for various research and measurement applications.

Input Channel

With its low-noise analog pre-amplifier, the signal input of DXA-003 can be switched to operate in single-ended or differential voltage mode. The input noise is exceptionally low at 6 nV/√Hz (@100 kHz). Users can select between 50 Ω or 10 MΩ input impedance, and the full-scale input voltage sensitivity covers a wide range from 1 nV to 1V. Two built-in line filters (50/60 Hz and 100/120 Hz) effectively eliminate line-related interference. A programmable gain amplifier adjusts the dynamic reserve according to the input signal magnitude, achieving an inherently large dynamic reserve up to 120dB. Signal digitization is performed by a precision 14-bit A/D converter sampling at 250MSPS, coupled with a specific filter designed to prevent aliasing.

Reference Channel

The DXA-003 can utilize an externally applied sine or square wave, or its own internally synthesized reference source. In internal reference mode, a high-precision stabilized oscillator and digital synthesis algorithm generate a sinewave output for multiplication with the input signal, resulting in virtually no reference phase noise. The digital phase-shifting technique allows phase adjustment with 0.001° resolution. The internal reference operates at a fixed frequency from 1 mHz to 10 MHz. The instrument also accepts external references, including sinewave and TTL logic signals. Both rising and trailing edges can trigger the internal Phase Locking Loop (PLL). Harmonic detection is supported based on the reference frequency, capable of measuring harmonics up to 32767 times the basic frequency, provided the harmonic frequency remains below the 10 MHz operational limit.

Digital Demodulator and Output Filter

The core component is the digital demodulator, which effectively rejects measurement errors caused by DC drift and offset commonly found in traditional analog lock-in amplifiers. Optimized multiplication within the digital demodulator minimizes calculation errors, enabling accurate detection of weak input signals. Selectable time constants for the output low-pass filter range from 30 ns to 4.4 ks, with roll-off options of 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, and 48 dB/oct. This filter is implemented using a high-performance digital filter sampled at 250 MHz. The combination of digital demodulation and the low-pass filter ensures a high dynamic reserve (>120dB) and accurate phase measurement (absolute phase error <1 deg). Furthermore, for input signal frequencies below 200 Hz, an optional synchronous filter can eliminate harmonic influences from the reference signal, allowing rapid and effective detection of low-frequency signals.

Display & Remote Operation

Display

DXA-003 features a 5.6-inch 640 x 480 color TFT-LCD. Measurement results like X, Y, R, and θ are displayed numerically and via bar graphs. An X-Y chart mode shows measurement trends over time, with a knob-controlled cursor for value checking.

Remote Operation

Users can control the DXA-003 via PC using various communication interfaces. This allows setting parameters and reading measurement data remotely. The instrument comes with a free LabVIEW program, simplifying its integration into complex scientific experiments.
