3D Gauss Meter Specifications (Model: DX-350)

Overview

The DX-350 3D Gauss Meter adopts advanced ARM microprocessing technology and uses multi-channel synchronous acquisition and processing technology to simultaneously display the magnetic induction intensity in the x, y, and z directions, and automatically calculate and display the Bxyz vector superposition value.

It is a three-channel high-precision digital gaussmeter developed for magnetic material production, scientific research institutes, aerospace and other units.

Device Images

Key Features

  • With 3.5-inch TFT true color screen, 7-digit display, the minimum resolution reaches 0.01G under the 30000G range.
  • Automatically displays the N/S polarity of the magnetic field.
  • Switch to mT, Gs, KA/m, Oe, T with one click.
  • One-click clearing function, no manual adjustment required.
  • Automatic peak measurement and hold functions.
  • Standard USB communication interface, optional software to form an automatic measurement system.
  • Optional automation interface (can be designed to match customer needs), suitable for secondary development.
