Liquid Nitrogen Cryostat - Atmosphere Type

Atmospheric liquid nitrogen cryostat: The sample can be placed in static helium so that the sample itself can be fully cooled. This design can evenly cool samples with poor thermal conductivity and difficulty in thermal anchoring (eg: liquids, powders, and some irregularly shaped samples).

Features

  • Temperature range (80K~325K), (optional 475K) has good cooling effect and the sample is heated evenly;
  • Equipped with funnel attachment, which can quickly refill refrigerant;
  • The sample is placed in a static helium atmosphere, which is suitable for cooling samples with irregular shapes and low thermal conductivity;
  • The temperature sensor uses PT100 platinum resistor with good stability and repeatability, one is installed on the heat exchanger and the sample holder respectively;
  • Top loading allows rapid replacement of samples;
  • Built-in charcoal to improve vacuum;
  • Can be customized according to customer requirements;
  • Equipped with 8 measuring leads as standard, which can be expanded to 32.
