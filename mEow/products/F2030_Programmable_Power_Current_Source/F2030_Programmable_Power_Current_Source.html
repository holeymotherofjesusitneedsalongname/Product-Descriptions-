F2030 Programmable Power Current Source - Overview

Product Overview

The F2030 is a high-performance, programmable DC current source designed for precision applications. It provides a stable and accurate current output from -10A to +10A with high resolution (0.1mA). Key features include its ability to drive inductive loads effectively using a linear current ramp, controlled current direction reversal, and multiple automation interfaces (RS-232 and USB).

It incorporates features for operational safety and instrument protection, including voltage compliance limits, internal overpower protection, and controlled output states. The floating output design offers flexibility in grounding configurations.

Key Features

  • Wide Output Range: -10.0000 A to +10.0000 A
  • High Resolution: 0.1 mA
  • Good Accuracy: ±(0.05% setting + 2 mA)
  • Excellent Stability: 0.02% of setting (@ 10A)
  • High Compliance Voltage: Up to 100 V DC during operation
  • Low Noise: < 1 mA RMS typical
  • Floating Output: Flexible grounding options
  • Linear Current Ramp: Adjustable rate (0.01 to 2 A/s) minimizes overshoot
  • Controlled Current Reversal: Relay-based with programmable delays
  • Current Sweep Functionality: 3 modes (SWA, SWB, SWC) up to 10A
  • Inductive Load Handling: Internal compensation network
  • Dual Computer Interfaces: RS-232 and USB (via internal converter)
  • Trigger Output: BNC for synchronization
  • Protection Mechanisms: Voltage compliance, internal overpower (350W), controllable output state
  • Clear Front Panel: LCD display, LED indicators, keypad control
  • Rack Mountable: 3U full rack size

Important Operational Notes

  • High Voltage Hazard: Output voltage can reach 100V during operation and up to 125V open circuit. Exercise caution when connecting/disconnecting loads. Use the High Impedance output state for safety.
  • Load Matching: Ensure load resistance is appropriate for the desired current, respecting the 100V compliance limit (e.g., < 10Ω for 10A). Minimum recommended resistance is 3Ω.
  • Inductive Loads: Use appropriate (often slower) ramp rates and sufficient reversal delay times to prevent overshoot and protect the instrument. Consider external compensation for loads with time constants > 2 seconds.
  • Grounding: The output is floating. For defined voltage reference and safety, connect *one* output terminal (either Red or Black) to the green safety ground terminal.
  • Output Wiring: Use heavy gauge wire (at least 4 sq mm recommended) for the output terminals, especially at higher currents (near 10A), to prevent cable overheating.
  • Cooling: Ensure adequate ventilation space around the unit as specified. Do not block air inlets or outlets.
  • USB Connection: Requires installation of the provided Prolific USB-to-Serial driver on the control computer.
  • Warm-Up: Allow 30 minutes for the instrument to stabilize for optimal performance and accuracy.
  • Weight: The unit is heavy (25-30 kg). Use appropriate lifting techniques (2 persons recommended) and ensure the support surface (desk/rack) can handle the weight.
