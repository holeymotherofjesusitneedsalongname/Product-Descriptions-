Liquid Nitrogen (Helium) Cryostat

Continuous Flow - Dexinmag

Overview

The Dexinmag continuous flow liquid nitrogen (helium) cryostat is designed primarily for cooling liquids, powders, and samples with poor thermal conductivity or irregular shapes. It is particularly effective for samples that are difficult to thermally anchor.

In operation, low-pressure refrigerant (Liquid Nitrogen or Helium) enters the thermostat's interior via the infusion pipe and liquid inlet. It then passes through a pre-laid low-temperature heat leakage capillary to reach the evaporation amplifier. A heater and temperature sensor are installed on the evaporator. The resulting temperature-controlled cold vapor (steam) is injected into the sample chamber to cool the sample.

After cooling the sample, the vapor is discharged either to the atmosphere through the air outlet or into a gas recovery system for secondary utilization. This model features top-loading for samples. Its compact overall design facilitates integration with magnets and brackets produced by Dexinmag, allowing for placement within strong magnetic fields while maximizing the usable field strength.

Functions and Features

  • Variable temperature range: (4K-325K), (80K-325K), (optional 475K) with good cooling effect and even sample heating.
  • Suitable for cooling samples with irregular shapes and low thermal conductivity.
  • Temperature Sensors:
    • Silicon diode sensor for 4K-325K range.
    • PT100 platinum resistance sensor for 80K-325K range (good stability and repeatability).
    • Cernox™ magnetic field insensitive temperature sensors suitable for use in magnetic fields.
  • Top loading enables rapid sample change.
  • Built-in charcoal getter to improve vacuum insulation.
  • Customizable according to customer requirements.
  • Comes standard with 8 measurement leads, expandable up to 32.
  • Detachable stainless steel infusion lines connect the cryostat to the Dewar, featuring a needle valve for flow control.
