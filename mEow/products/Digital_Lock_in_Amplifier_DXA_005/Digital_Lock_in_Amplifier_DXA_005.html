Digital Lock-In Amplifier DXA-005-DSP

Overview

The DXA-005 digital lock-in amplifier is the latest core technology product launched, with excellent high performance and broadband measurement capabilities. This instrument is based on digital modulation technology and is equipped with a 24 bit high-precision analog-to-digital converter (ADC) and a 16 bit high-speed analog-to-digital converter (DAC). With the newly launched stream architecture, it can accurately, quickly, and flexibly detect effective signal components hidden in strong noise.

DXA-005 not only measures the amplitude and phase information of input signals simultaneously, but also achieves international leading levels in key performance indicators such as measurement accuracy, operating frequency range, signal-to-noise ratio, and dynamic reserve. In addition, the newly added multi harmonic measurement, oscilloscope and spectrum analyzer functions, and PID control functions make OE2021 widely applicable to various needs in scientific research and industrial fields.

Key Features

  • Measure frequency range from 10 μHz to 1.5MHz
  • Input noise as low as 2.5nV/√Hz

Input Channel

The DXA-005 is equipped with a low-noise analog front-end amplifier that can efficiently process differential or single ended signals, with equivalent input noise as low as 2.5nV/√Hz. The input impedance of this channel is 10M Ω, and the full-scale sensitivity range is 1nVrms to 5Vrms. In addition, the signal input channel adopts high-precision 24 bit ADC, and the dynamic range can reach over 130dB.

Output Channel

DXA-005 is a 16 bit analog-to-digital converter (DAC) based on 32 MSPS, capable of generating high-precision sine wave signals in the frequency range of 10 μHz to 1.5MHz, and adjustable DC bias. The signal amplitude range is 0.1Vrms to 5Vrms, and the DC bias range is ± 5Vdc. For external devices that require bias voltage, such as electro-optic modulators, OE2021, with its excellent driving capability, can directly drive the device without the need for additional level conversion amplifiers. The phase of the output signal is synchronized with the internal oscillator of the instrument, and the phase offset can be independently set. In addition, DXA-005 supports AM/FM/PM modulation function, making it convenient for users to modulate and control the system.

Reference Channel

The reference signal of the lock-in amplifier can be selected as sine wave or square wave signal according to the actual needs of the user, or it can be a reference signal synthesized internally by the instrument. When DXA-005 is set to internal reference signal mode, the high-precision oscillator and digital synthesis algorithm inside the instrument will generate a sine wave signal for multiplication with the input signal, and the internal reference signal is almost unaffected by phase noise. Through digital phase shifting technology, the phase resolution of the internal reference signal can reach 1 µ deg.

When DXA-005 operates in external reference signal mode, it can accept sine wave signals or TTL logic levels as external reference signals, which will be locked by an internal digital phase-locked loop. Based on the frequency of the reference signal, DXA-005 can detect signals of the same frequency and its harmonics, up to 10000 harmonics of the fundamental frequency, but the maximum harmonic frequency cannot exceed the upper limit of the instrument's measurement bandwidth.

Digital Demodulator

DXA-005 has 8 synchronous decoders, each of which can be independently controlled. The DXA-005 time constant can be flexibly set within the range of 100ns to 3ks, and users can customize the time constant according to their needs. The steep drop rate of the filter can be selected from 6-48 dB/oct, with a total of 8 orders. Using digital modulation technology and advanced filter structure, DXA-005 has higher dynamic reserve (>130dB), more accurate phase (absolute phase error<1°), zero DC drift, and excellent orthogonal performance compared to traditional analog lock-in amplifiers. In addition, DXA-005 provides optional synchronous filters that can quickly eliminate the influence of signal harmonics, ensuring that the instrument can accurately detect low-frequency signals while responding quickly.

Display & Communication Interface

Color Display Screen

The DXA-005 is equipped with a 5.6-inch TFT color display screen with a resolution of 640 × 480, which serves as the main display interface of the instrument and can be independently controlled in all directions through a keyboard. On the display screen, users can flexibly view the X, Y, R, θ and other parameters of the demodulator, and also configure various basic settings such as filter constants, making the operation intuitive and convenient.

Communication Interface

DXA-005 has USB 2.0, 1000Mbps RJ45 Ethernet port, WIFI wireless network interface, RS232 serial port, and GPIB interface. Through various communication interfaces, users can effectively use all testing functions of DXA-005 on the control computer, set reasonable control parameters for the instrument, and read the data measured by the instrument.

  • USB 2.0: 480 Mbps high-speed interface
  • Ethernet: RJ45-1000Mbps wired interface
  • WIFI wireless network interface
  • GPIB: IEEE-488.2 interface
  • RS-232: Standard 9-pin RS-232 female socket
